## What the project is
The FF1 Game Boy port is a hand-written native SM83 engine in `tools/gbn/` (`src/*.s`, assembled
by `tools/gb/asm_sm83.py`). Every game byte comes from the user's NES ROM at build time.

- Build: `python3 tools/gbn/build.py --out "build/FF1 native (English, GB-GBC).gb"`.
- Test options: `--battle F` (a battle right after New Game) and `--start MAP,X,Y[,ROOM]`.
- Test in SameBoy, using the harness in the session scratchpad (`pt2/play.py`, `play/drv.py`,
  `pol/*.py`). These scripts are scratch files, not in the repo; a new session must write its
  own.

## Owner's request (as it stood at handoff time)
1. "The ocean stops and restarts without holding its last known position."
2. "Add the ocean effect to tiles in towns."

### Cause of (1)
`wa_tick` (`tools/gbn/src/wanim.s`) took the water's step from the global `frame` counter.
A map load (including the reload after a menu or a battle) puts the water tiles back at their
first picture, and only the slots that change at each step were rewritten afterwards. The sea
therefore jumped: back to the start, then to wherever `frame` had got to.

### What is written (uncommitted at handoff time; now committed as "WIP water")
- `src/wanim.s`:
  - `wa_t`: the water's own step counter. It advances only while water is shown, so menus,
    battles and loads hold the water in place.
  - `wa_ok`: cleared by `load_map` (one line added in `src/main.s`, after `ld [q_rd], a`).
  - `wa_full`: after a load, `wa_ow` redraws every animated slot at the current step
    (`wa_msk` returns "changes" for all slots while it is set).
  - New code placed in free bank 0 gaps: `$13CB` (`wa_ow`, `wa_step`), `$2F52` (`wa_town`),
    `$31CE` (`wt_cp`). `wa_msk` moved to `$1459`.
  - New WRAM at `$C6E8`.
- Towns (maps 0-7, tileset 0): the water is the single tile `$0F` (metatiles 39, 40, 41, 59).
  - `wa_town` animates it outside rooms: 64 steps, two bands of 4 lines, each moving a pixel
    sideways per 16 frames.
  - `wt_cp` copies the step's 16 bytes to VRAM in V-Blank, using the existing `memcpy`.
- `tools/gbn/build.py` `town_water()` fills data bank `WTOWN_BANK = 109`:
  - `$4400 + 2*entry`: the tile's VRAM address.
  - `$4800 + $400*entry`: 64 pictures per entry.
  - Entries 0-7 are the towns: the DMG tile, pre-shaded per map (taken from
    `map_pack()['mt_dmg_out'][39]`).
  - Entry 8 is the Color, at `$90F0`.
- The build assembles; nothing has been run yet.
