## Classes (まじゅつし 魔術師 vs まどうし 魔導師)
せんし Warrior, シーフ Thief, モンク Monk, あか/しろ/くろまじゅつし Red/White/Black Magician →
ナイト Knight, ニンジャ Ninja, スーパーモンク Super Monk, あか/しろ/くろまどうし Red/White/Black Wizard.
The Japanese keeps 魔術師 (sorcery practitioner) and 魔導師 (master of magic) distinct; Magician → Wizard mirrors that.

## Monsters
かいぞく "Pirate" and パイレーツ "Pirates" are separate monsters in the Japanese and are kept apart.
ホワイトダイル is kept as "White Dile". かくこうげき (enemy attack) = 核攻撃 "Nuclear Attack".

## Style
- Speech written entirely in katakana (robots, Kraken) is rendered in ALL CAPS.
- The dwarf greeting ラリホー is rendered "Lali-ho!".
- Dialogue 120 (とくれせん / たぼーび) is "B button and select" written backwards; the English keeps the puzzle ("tceles dna / nottub B").
