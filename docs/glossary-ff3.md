## Things
ふゆうそうのくつ Floating Shoes (ふゆうそう is ambiguous in kana: 浮遊草 "floating grass" or 浮遊装
"floating gear"; the shoes let you walk over the bottomless swamp, rendered "Floating Shoes") ·
しょうごう titles (称号: the job titles the Crystals grant) · みずのみこ Maiden of Water ·
